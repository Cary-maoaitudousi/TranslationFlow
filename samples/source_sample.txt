Chapter 100: Arc 3 - chapter 15

Chapter Text

The slow breeze carried the smell of rain through the ferns, and Mistpaw lifted her nose to taste it. Somewhere beyond the ridge, ThunderClan's border markers were turning sour, and she knew that Firestar would want to hear about it before moonhigh.

She picked her way down the slope, careful not to disturb the loose stones. Frostfur had told her a hundred times that a patrol was only as quiet as its loudest cat, and Mistpaw did not intend to be that cat tonight.

At the bottom of the hollow, Thistleclaw was waiting. The old warrior sat with his tail wrapped around his paws, his eyes half closed, as though the whole forest had nothing better to do than wait for him.

"You're late," Thistleclaw said.

"I'm not late. The border moved."

His ears flicked. "Borders don't move, apprentice. Cats do."

Mistpaw bit back the answer that rose in her throat. Sandstorm had warned her about that too: never argue with a warrior who has already decided what he saw.

They walked together toward Highrock, where the stones still held the day's warmth. Graystripe was there, and Sootkit, who should have been in the nursery but never was.

"ShadowClan scent on the far side," Mistpaw said. "Fresh. Two cats, maybe three."

Graystripe's whiskers twitched. "Then we go and look. All four of us."

"No," said Sootkit, and every cat turned. "Five. I'm coming."

Chapter 101: Arc 3 - Chapter 16

The hollow was practically buzzing when Mistpaw pushed through the thorn tunnel. Cats crowded the clearing shoulder to shoulder, and the air smelled of wet moss and worry.

Reedstar stood on the Speaking Stone with his chin lifted, and beside him Ashenwater's medicine cat held a bundle of yarrow in her teeth like a challenge.

"We will not be the Clan that forgets," Reedstar said. "Not this leaf-bare."

A murmur ran around the clearing. Mistpaw found Frostfur near the apprentices' den, and the older she-cat pressed her muzzle briefly against her cheek.

"Whatever happens tonight," Frostfur murmured, "you keep your claws sheathed until I say otherwise."

Beyond the camp wall, the river slid past Amber Hollow, black and patient. Mistpaw watched it until the moon rose over the ridge, and somewhere out in that darkness a cat she did not know was watching her back.
